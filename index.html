Kinetic Conditioning (K-HIIT)

Interval Clock

ROUND 1/3

PREPARE

00:45

Medical Overrides

1. Warm Up (Primer)

Joint Prep & Mobility

The Setup Light Kettlebell

The Execution

KB Halo to Goblet Squat. Orbit KB tightly around the head. Drop immediately into a deep squat. Drive up and reverse direction.

The Shepherd Cue

"Draw a tight halo over your head, then sit straight down an invisible wall."

Safety Gate

Rib flare during the halo (lumbar extension compensation).

RA OVERRIDE: WARM UP

Execution

Unweighted Shoulder Rolls to Wall Squat. Eliminate grip load. Use wall support to eliminate knee shear.

Safety Gate

Dropping hips below 90 degrees on the wall, stressing the knee capsule.

2. Block A (Sagittal Power)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

The Setup Heavy DB / Ankle Weights

The Execution

M1: Heavy Goblet Squat. 3-second eccentric descent. 1-second explosive concentric drive.

M2: Alternating Reverse Lunge. Step back into deep lunge, explode forward. Alternate legs every rep for 45s.

The Shepherd Cue

"Lock your core into concrete before you explode upward."

Safety Gate

Knee valgus (caving inward) on the lunge drive.

RA OVERRIDE: BLOCK A

Execution

M1: Med Ball Wall Squat. M2: Static Split Squat. Eliminate dynamic stepping (shearing force) and heavy finger grips.

Safety Gate

Front knee pushing past the toes in the static split squat.

3. Block B (Transverse Core)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

The Setup Medium DBs / Light DB

The Execution

M1: Alternating Renegade Row. High plank. Row one arm back, alternate arms every rep. Fight rotation.

M2: Weighted Hollow Hold. Supine. Static hold with light DB extended over chest.

The Shepherd Cue

"Balance a glass of water on your lower back during the row."

Safety Gate

Hips swinging side-to-side during the row (Regress to knees).

RA OVERRIDE: BLOCK B

Execution

M1: Standing Banded Row. M2: Supine Dead Bug. Eliminate bearing body weight on extended wrists in the plank.

Safety Gate

Lumbar spine popping off the mat during the Dead Bug.

4. Finisher (Burnout)

2 Minutes Unbroken

The Setup Heavy Dumbbells

The Execution

Bilateral Farmer's Carry. Hold heavy DBs in both hands. Walk continuously for 2 minutes maintaining perfect posture.

The Shepherd Cue

"Walk like you are carrying heavy suitcases through a narrow hallway."

Safety Gate

Shrugging shoulders into the ears as grip fatigue sets in.

RA OVERRIDE: FINISHER

Execution

Med Ball Bear Hug Carry. Hug a heavy medicine ball to the chest. Protects the small finger joints from tearing under heavy DB load.

Safety Gate

Leaning backward (lumbar extension) to support the weight of the ball.

5. Warm Down (Integration)

Parasympathetic Flush

The Setup Bodyweight Mat

The Execution

Flowing Down Dog to Cobra. Slow, continuous spinal flush. Drop heart rate.

The Shepherd Cue

"Move slowly like a wave rolling back into the ocean."

Safety Gate

Pinching in the lower back during Cobra.

RA OVERRIDE: WARM DOWN

Execution

Child's Pose to Quadruped Cat/Cow. Eliminates bearing body weight on extended wrists required in Down Dog/Cobra.

Safety Gate

Forcing end-range flexion if hips or knees are inflamed.
