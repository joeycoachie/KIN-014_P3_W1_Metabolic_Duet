Phase 3: Kinetic Power (K-HIIT)

Interval Clock

ROUND 1/3

PREPARE

00:45

Warm Up

Dynamic Flow Mapping

The Setup Mat / Bodyweight

The Execution

Continuous flowing sequence: Downward Pike -> Articulate to Plank -> Step through to Deep Squat -> Return to Pike.

The Shepherd Cue

"Move like a continuous wave crashing and retreating. No pauses."

The Safety Gate

Lumbar spine rounding (butt wink) at the absolute bottom of the deep squat.

RA OVERRIDE ACTIVATED

Modified Execution: Quadruped Cat/Cow transitioning to a shallow kneeling lunge.

Clinical Reason: Removes extreme wrist extension (Plank) and deep knee flexion (Squat) to protect inflamed joint capsules.

Block A (Sagittal Power)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

The Setup Heavy Dumbbell

The Execution

M1: Goblet Squat. 3-second slow descent, explosive drive up.

M2: Reverse Lunge. R1: Isolate Right Leg. R2: Isolate Left Leg. R3: Alternate legs every rep.

The Shepherd Cue

"Don't just stand up. Push the floor completely away from you."

The Safety Gate

Knee valgus (caving inward) on the lunge drive, indicating glute medius failure.

RA OVERRIDE ACTIVATED

Modified M1: Box Squat. Sit entirely onto a box/bench, then stand.

Modified M2: Static Split Squat (Do not step backward dynamically).

Clinical Reason: Box squats limit depth to 90 degrees protecting the knee capsule. Static squats eliminate the high-impact deceleration of stepping.

Block B (Transverse Core)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

The Setup Medium Dumbbells

The Execution

M1: Renegade Row. High plank on DBs. Row alternating arms. Resist rotation.

M2: Hollow Body Hold. Supine isometric hold. Legs at 45 degrees, arms extended.

The Shepherd Cue

"Lock your pelvis in concrete. If you row your right arm, drive your right toe into the floor."

The Safety Gate

Hips swinging side-to-side during the row (Regress to knees immediately).

RA OVERRIDE ACTIVATED

Modified M1: Forearm Plank Hold (Ditch the row).

Modified M2: Dead Bug (Knees bent to tabletop, alternating heel taps).

Clinical Reason: Removes severe wrist extension from the Renegade Row. Dead Bug shortens the lever to protect the lumbar spine.

Block C (Frontal Plane)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

The Setup Medium Dumbbells

The Execution

M1: Unilateral Push Press. R1: Right Arm. R2: Left Arm. R3: Bilateral (Both Arms). Use a micro-dip in legs to drive weight up.

M2: Lateral Lunge. R1: Right Side. R2: Left Side. R3: Alternate sides. Keep trailing leg perfectly straight.

The Shepherd Cue

"Punch a hole directly into the ceiling. Don't let your ribs flare open."

The Safety Gate

Lateral spine flexion (leaning away from the pressing arm) indicating core failure under load.

RA OVERRIDE ACTIVATED

Modified M1: Seated Neutral-Grip DB Press (Palms facing each other).

Modified M2: Standing Lateral Band Walks (Use loop band around thighs).

Clinical Reason: Seated press stabilizes the spine and neutral grip protects the shoulder capsule. Band walks remove knee sheer from the deep lateral lunge.

Warm Down

Systemic Integration

The Setup Mat

The Execution

Supine Spinal Twist into Active Hamstring Sweeps. Slow the breathing rate. Passive hold for 60 seconds per side.

The Shepherd Cue

"Melt completely into the floor. Let gravity do all the remaining work."

The Safety Gate

Forcing the twist so far that the opposite shoulder blade peels off the mat.

RA OVERRIDE ACTIVATED

Modified Execution: Keep both knees bent and stacked during the twist. Place a block between the knees if needed.

Clinical Reason: Stacking the knees reduces the rotational lever arm, protecting the SI joint and lower back from excessive torque.
