Warm-Up (Mobility)

KB Halo into Goblet Squat

1. The Setup Light Kettlebell Standing tall, core braced.

2. The Execution

  • Orbit KB tightly around head (Halo).
  • Drop immediately into a deep goblet squat.
  • Drive up and reverse halo direction.

RA Modification (Joint Protect)

Drop the weight entirely. Perform a pain-free range bodyweight box squat to limit knee flexion. Replace the halo with unweighted shoulder rolls.

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Orbit the weight like a halo, then drop your hips straight down like an elevator shaft."

4. Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Rib flare during the halo, or lumbar spine rounding (butt wink) at the bottom of the squat.

Block A (Sagittal Power)

Alternating Load

1. The Setup 1 Heavy Dumbbell Neutral spine, wide stance.

2. The Execution

Movement 1: Goblet Squat

3-second slow eccentric descent. 1-second explosive concentric drive.

Movement 2: Alternating Reverse Lunge

Step back into deep lunge, explode forward. Alternate legs continuously.

RA Modification (Joint Protect)

M1: Goblet Squat onto a physical bench (restricts deep flexion). M2: Replace deep lunges with Alternating Reverse Step-Taps (do not drop the back knee).

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Explode through the floor to stand, but fight gravity all the way down."

4. Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Knee valgus (caving inward) on the lunge drive, indicating glute medius failure.

Block B (Transverse Core)

Anti-Rotation & Isometrics

1. The Setup Medium DBs / Mat High plank position, wide feet.

2. The Execution

Movement 1: Alternating Renegade Row

High plank. Row right arm, row left arm. Fight rotational pull.

Movement 2: Weighted Hollow Hold

Supine. Legs at 45 deg. Hold single DB over chest. Static hold.

RA Modification (Joint Protect)

CRITICAL: High plank risks severe wrist compression. M1: Move to Kneeling Banded Rows. M2: Replace Hollow Hold with Supine Dead Bugs (head resting on mat).

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Lock your hips in concrete. Row the weight without spilling the glass of water on your back."

4. Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Hips swinging violently side-to-side during the row, or lumbar spine arching off the mat.

Warm-Down (Unwind)

Regulated Flush

1. The Setup Mat Only Seated, legs extended wide.

2. The Execution

  • Spine Stretch Forward.
  • Rotate into The Saw (R/L).
  • Transition smoothly into Z-Sit Mermaid stretch.

RA Modification (Joint Protect)

If hips are flared/inflamed, Z-sit on the floor is contraindicated. Move client to a physical chair for seated lateral reaches.

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Paint a massive circle around your body, letting your heart rate drop with every exhale."

4. Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Lifting the opposing sit-bones off the mat during the lateral stretch.
