Metabolic Pilates (Gym Floor)

Session Clock

00:00

The Primer (Mobility Flow)

Gym Floor Warm-Up

LANE A: ZI XUEN (Dynamic)

  • World's Greatest Stretch
  • Deep Lunge with Thoracic Rotation

LANE B: MAE (RA Safe)

  • Standing Spinal Roll Downs
  • Walk out to Wall Push-Ups (Fists/Neutral Wrists)

The Wolf Cue

Elevate heart rate via full-body multi-joint movement. Lubricate joints before load.

The Shepherd Cue

"Move like a continuous engine warming up. Don't stop moving."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Mae putting full body weight on flat, extended wrists on the floor.

The Anchor (Leg Burn)

45s Work / 15s Transition

LANE A: ZI XUEN (Plyometric)

  • Jump Squats (45 seconds)
  • Isometric Squat Holds (15 seconds)

LANE B: MAE (Low Impact)

  • Static Sumo Squats (Heavy DB at chest)
  • Focus on depth and glute activation

The Wolf Cue

Glute dominance. Monitor Zi Xuen's hip stability during the landing phase.

The Shepherd Cue

"The floor is lava. Drop your weight deep into your heels."

CRITICAL Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Valgus knee collapse (knees caving inward) during fatigue.

The Crucible (Core Matrix)

Continuous Mat Flow

LANE A: ZI XUEN (Dynamic Core)

  • Full Plank Jacks
  • Mountain Climbers (High pace)

LANE B: MAE (Static Core)

  • Forearm Plank Holds (Protects Wrists)
  • Forearm Side Planks (Knees down if needed)

The Wolf Cue

Anti-extension in the sagittal plane. Zero lumbar sagging while the limbs move.

The Shepherd Cue

"Balance a glass of water on your lower back. Don't spill it."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Mae reporting any cervical spine (neck) or wrist pressure. Drop to knees immediately.

The Integration (Unwind)

Static Cooldown

LANE A: ZI XUEN

  • Deep Pigeon Pose (Floor)

LANE B: MAE

  • Seated Figure 4 Stretch (Protects knees from deep flexion)

The Wolf Cue

Parasympathetic down-regulation. Focus on exhales to slow the heart rate.

The Shepherd Cue

"Melt heavily into the floor. Exhale the tension away."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Forcing external hip rotation past the point of knee discomfort.
