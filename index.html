Warm-Up (Mobility)

Dynamic Joint Lubrication

1. The Setup

Standing. Light Kettlebell (Goblet grip).

Standing. Bodyweight only (No KB). Wide stance.

2. The Execution (45s Flow)

• Orbit KB tightly around the head (Halo).

• Drop immediately into a deep goblet squat.

• Drive up and reverse the halo direction.

• Large arm circles overhead (No weight).

• Drop into a partial range quarter-squat (90 degrees max).

• Return to standing, maintaining vertical spine.

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Orbit the weight, then drop your hips straight down like a glass elevator."

"Draw massive circles with your fingertips. Only sit as deep as a high bar stool."

4. The Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Rib flare during the halo, or lumbar spine rounding (butt wink) at the bottom of the deep squat.

Pushing into joint pain during the squat. If knees ache, regress depth immediately.

Block A (Sagittal Power)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

1. The Setup

Heavy DB (Goblet) / Ankle Weights on.

TRX Suspension Straps or secure barre for hand support.

2. The Execution

M1: Goblet Squat. 3-sec slow descent. Explosive concentric drive up.

M2: Reverse Lunge + Knee Drive. Step back into deep lunge, explode forward driving the knee to chest height.

M1: TRX Assisted Squat. Use arms to offload knee weight. Slow, controlled partial range.

M2: Static Split Squat. Hold TRX. Maintain static lunge stance, drop back knee straight down (No explosive knee drive).

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Load the spring slowly, then drive the floor away from you violently."

"Use the straps to make yourself 20% lighter. Move like you are moving through water."

4. The Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Knee valgus (caving inward) on the lunge drive, indicating glute medius failure.

Locking out the knees sharply at the top of the squat. Keep a micro-bend to protect the joint.

Block B (Transverse Core)

45s Work / 15s Rest | 3 Rounds

1. The Setup

High plank position. Medium DBs under hands. / Supine for M2.

Quadruped (All fours) on padded mat. No weights (protect wrists).

2. The Execution

M1: Renegade Row. High plank. Row one arm back while fighting rotational pull.

M2: Weighted Hollow Hold. Supine. Legs at 45 degrees. Hold DB extended over chest.

M1: Quadruped Bird-Dog. Extend opposite arm and leg slowly. Focus on spinal stabilization, not load.

M2: Dead Bug. Supine. Head resting on mat. Extend opposite arm/leg without weights.

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Balance a hot cup of coffee on your pelvis during the row. Do not spill it."

"Press your lower back into the mat like you are squishing a grape during the Dead Bug."

4. The Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Hips swinging violently side-to-side during the row, or lumbar spine arching in Hollow Hold.

Wrist pain in quadruped (Regress to forearms immediately if RA flare-up occurs).

Burnout (Finisher)

2 Minutes Continuous

1. The Setup

Standing. Heaviest KB/DB in one hand.

Standing. Light DBs held in front rack (at shoulders) to avoid grip fatigue.

2. The Execution

Unilateral Farmer's Carry. Walk for 60 seconds with weight in right hand. Switch hands immediately for final 60 seconds.

Bilateral Front Rack Carry. Walk for 2 minutes with light weights resting on shoulders (eliminates traction stress on elbow/finger joints).

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Walk like you are carrying heavy luggage through a narrow hallway without hitting the walls."

"Walk tall, keeping your collarbones wide and your breath steady."

4. The Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Leaning heavily to the side to compensate for the unilateral weight. Shrugging shoulder into ear.

Arching the lower back to support the front rack weights.

Warm Down (Cool Down)

Parasympathetic Reset

1. The Setup

Mat. Bodyweight.

Mat. Bodyweight. Props (Pillows/Blocks) available.

2. The Execution

Child's Pose into Thread the Needle. Sit hips back to heels. Slide one arm under the chest to stretch posterior shoulder.

Static Hamstring Stretch. Supine. Single leg extended.

Supported Child's Pose. Place block under forehead. Avoid deep knee flexion if inflamed.

Gentle Knee Sway. Supine. Knees bent, feet flat. Slowly sway knees side to side (Windshield Wipers).

3. The Shepherd Cue

"Melt your chest into the floor. Inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6 seconds to slow the heart rate."

"Let the mat completely support your weight. Do not push into any stretch."

4. The Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Forcing the rotational stretch instead of breathing into it.

Holding breath due to joint discomfort.
