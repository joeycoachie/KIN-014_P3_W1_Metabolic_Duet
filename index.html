Warm Up (The Primer)

Standard Protocol

Setup

Standing. Light Kettlebell held at chest.

Execution

Orbit KB tightly around the head (Halo), drop immediately into a deep goblet squat. Drive up and reverse.

Shepherd Cue

"Draw a tight halo of light around your crown, then drop like an elevator."

Safety Gate

Rib flare during the halo; lumbar rounding (butt wink) in the deep squat.

Medical Override: Rheumatoid Arthritis

Setup

Seated on box. Bodyweight only. Zero wrist loading.

Execution

Seated Thoracic Rotations (arms crossed). Transition to slow, supported Sit-to-Stands (no deep flexion).

Shepherd Cue

"Wring out your chest gently. Stand up smoothly using your glutes, not momentum."

Safety Gate

Knees collapsing inward on the stand; using hands/wrists to push off the box.

Block A (Sagittal Power)

Standard Protocol

Setup

Movement 1 (M1): Heavy DB Goblet. Movement 2 (M2): Ankle Weights.

Execution

M1: 3-sec slow eccentric descent, explosive drive up.
M2: Reverse lunge, explode forward driving the knee to chest height.

Shepherd Cue

"Load the spring slowly, then explode off the floor."

Safety Gate

Knee valgus (caving inward) on the lunge drive; losing neutral spine at squat depth.

Medical Override: Rheumatoid Arthritis

Setup

M1: Bodyweight Box Squat. M2: Static Supported Split Squat.

Execution

Eliminate all explosive shear force. M1: Controlled tap to box. M2: Hold TRX or wall for balance, drop straight down slowly.

Shepherd Cue

"Move like you are underwater. Constant, smooth tension."

Safety Gate

Any impact or bouncing at the bottom of the movement.

Block B (Transverse Core)

Standard Protocol

Setup

M1: High Plank with DBs. M2: Supine on Mat with 1 Light DB.

Execution

M1 (Renegade Row): Row one arm back fighting rotational pull.
M2 (Hollow Hold): Static isometric hold, DB extended over chest.

Shepherd Cue

"Balance a glass of water on your lower back. Do not let it spill."

Safety Gate

Hips swinging side-to-side during the row; lumbar spine arching off mat in Hollow Hold.

Medical Override: Rheumatoid Arthritis

Setup

M1: Standing with Resistance Band. M2: Dead Bug on Mat (No weights).

Execution

Remove weight-bearing from inflamed wrists. M1: Standing Band Row (loop band around wrists if grip is painful). M2: Slow, unweighted alternating leg extensions.

Shepherd Cue

"Pull from your back, let your hands just be hooks."

Safety Gate

Death-gripping the band causing hand pain; straining the neck off the mat.

Warm Down (Integration)

Standard Protocol

Setup

Mat. Bodyweight.

Execution

Active floor stretching. Downward Dog into Pigeon Pose. Hold end ranges to flush lactic acid.

Shepherd Cue

"Sink into the floor. Let gravity do the work you just fought against."

Safety Gate

Holding breath to force a deeper stretch; misaligned hips in Pigeon.

Medical Override: Rheumatoid Arthritis

Setup

Supine on Mat. Pillows/Blocks available.

Execution

Supported parasympathetic breathing. Gentle 90/90 hip shifts (windshield wipers). Avoid all end-range joint stretching to prevent flare-ups.

Shepherd Cue

"Move your legs side to side like seaweed in the ocean."

Safety Gate

Pushing a joint to the point of a 'stretch' sensation. Keep everything mid-range.
