Protocol

SYS-HIIT-001

Format: Gym Floor (Duet / RA Modified)

Session Clock

00:00

The Primer (Mobility Flow)

Universal Floor Prep

LANE A (Standard)

  • 90/90 Hip Switches (Floor)
  • Glute Bridges (Bodyweight)

LANE B (RA Modified)

  • 90/90 Hip Switches (Use hands for support)
  • Glute Bridges (Focus on articulation)

The Wolf Cue

Lubricate the synovial joints. Ensure the femur rotates cleanly within the acetabulum.

The Shepherd Cue

"Move like oil warming up in an engine. Smooth and deliberate."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Forcing range of motion through spinal compensation instead of true hip mobility.

The Anchor (Strength)

Heavy Isometric/Eccentric

LANE A (Standard)

  • Goblet Squat Hold (Kettlebell)
  • Alternating Reverse Lunges (Controlled)

LANE B (RA Modified)

  • Wall Sit (Bodyweight or Ankle Weights)
  • Standing March (Anti-rotation focus)

The Wolf Cue

Sustain deep muscle contraction to fatigue without causing joint shear or relying on momentum.

The Shepherd Cue

"Become an immovable statue. Let the muscles burn, keep the joints silent."

CRITICAL Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Knees caving inward (Valgus collapse) during the squat/lunge or wall sit.

The Crucible (Metabolic)

30s Work / 10s Transition

LANE A (Standard)

  • Dumbbell Thrusters (Squat to Press)
  • Floor Mountain Climbers

LANE B (RA Modified)

  • Shadow Boxing (Wearing Wrist Weights)
  • Standing High Knee Drives (Ankle Weights)

The Wolf Cue

Cardiovascular spiking via continuous peripheral blood flow shifting (upper body to lower body).

The Shepherd Cue

"Keep the fire burning. Do not let the engine stall during the transitions."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Lane A: Lumbar collapse in plank. Lane B: Gripping weights too tightly or bearing weight on flexed wrists.

The Integration (Unwind)

Strap-Assisted Release

UNIVERSAL MAT (Lane A & B)

  • Supine Strap-Assisted Hamstring Stretch
  • Open-Chest Breathing (Supine over rolled towel)

The Wolf Cue

Down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system. Passive eccentric release.

The Shepherd Cue

"Let the floor completely swallow your body weight."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Tensing the neck or shoulders to pull aggressively on the stretching straps.
